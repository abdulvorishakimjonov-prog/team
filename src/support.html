A Supportive Environment for Every Studen

Q-TEAM is the information hub where students get answers to their questions. Advice is free, impartial, non-judgemental and confidential. If a question needs another department, Q-TEAM refers the student there.

How Q-TEAM Can Help
01
Academic Support

Help with academic procedures, requirements, adapting to university standards, and resolving academic difficulties.

02
Orientation & Adaptation

Guidance on university services, campus rules, student life procedures, and adjusting to university routine.

03
Advice & Information

Information regarding Students' Union matters, complaints, finance questions, and general inquiries.

04
Wellbeing Support

Support in stressful situations, emotional and social wellbeing guidance, and referrals to professional services.

04
Advocacy & Feedback

Collecting student feedback, helping resolve interpersonal conflicts, and passing student suggestions directly to university administration.
