TOBB ETU in Tashkent

In particular, the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Uzbekistan signed a Memorandum on the opening of the Tashkent branch of the University of Economics and Technology of Turkey under the Union of Chambers of Commerce and Commodity Exchanges of Turkey "TOBB". It is worth to note that the initiative to open a branch of the University was put forward by the Uzbek side during the negotiations of the Business Council of Turkic-speaking states, held in October 2019 in Tashkent.
